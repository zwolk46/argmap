Stream E — Amendment 2 (UI Consolidation)

Amendment to stream_e_visual_polish_v1.html and stream_e_amendment_1.html  ·  Session: 2026-05-23 UI consolidation  ·  Date: 2026-05-23

Per Article XI § 1, stream documents are immutable. This second amendment records that the base palette + radius scale + shadow tokens in src/ui/styles/tokens.css have been collapsed onto the shadcn theme variables defined in src/index.css. The structural commitments in stream_e_uiux_v1.html and stream_e_amendment_1.html are not modified. The domain tokens calibrated in v1 (status palette, mode accents, edge families, severity, sub-flags, AI attribution, milestone, motion, z-index, type scale, spacing scale) remain authoritative in their own right and are preserved unchanged. What changes here is one design system replacing two parallel design systems: hand-rolled var(--color-surface-*) consumers and shadcn-based Tailwind utilities now resolve to identical pixel values.

Motivation

After the I.10b overhaul (amendment 1, 2026-05-20), the codebase carried two coexisting base-palette systems at :root:

  • The shadcn preset's pure-neutral OKLCH variables (--background: oklch(1 0 0), --foreground: oklch(0.145 0 0), --muted: oklch(0.97 0 0), --border: oklch(0.922 0 0), --radius: 0.625rem, etc.) — consumed by shadcn primitives via Tailwind utility classes (bg-background, text-foreground, border-input, rounded-md).
  • The legacy argmap warm-neutral HSL variables (--color-surface-canvas: hsl(36 16% 97%), --color-text-primary: hsl(30 8% 12%), --color-border-default: hsl(30 8% 80%), --radius-md: 6px, etc.) — consumed by hand-rolled CSS in global.css and by inline style={{ background: "var(--color-surface-pane)" }} in transitional surfaces (the topbar, three frame-building editors, the canvas-toolbar search input).

The result was visible inconsistency: pure-white shadcn dialogs/sheets/cards on one surface, slightly-warm cream pane backgrounds on another, in the same view. The user named this directly as the trigger for this session ("the design is kinda all over the place"). Additionally, hand-rolled var(--radius-md) resolved to 6px while shadcn rounded-md resolved to calc(var(--radius) * 0.8) = 8px — a 2px corner-radius mismatch between paired surfaces.

What changed

The base-palette tokens in src/ui/styles/tokens.css are now aliases to the shadcn theme variables defined in src/index.css. Both naming families coexist; the --color-* argmap names continue to resolve, but every base-palette token now derives from one OKLCH source of truth.

2. Domain tokens — PRESERVED unchanged

Article II § 5 makes the status palette constitutionally protected. Stream E amendment 1 separately commits to preserving the rest of the domain tokens. None of these are modified by this amendment:

  • Status palette: satisfied (hsl(145 36% 38%)), open, contested, foreclosed, not_applicable. The five-status canvas-node treatment in src/ui/styles/global.css via .canvas-node[data-status][data-kind] selectors is untouched.
  • Mode accents: frame-building slate-blue (hsl(216 28% 42%)), argument-running burnt sienna (hsl(18 38% 44%)).
  • Edge families: structural, foreclosure, supports, contradicts, answers, annotation.
  • Severity: pass, warning, error.
  • Legal sub-flags: binding navy, persuasive slate.
  • AI attribution: plum.
  • Milestone star: warm gold.
  • Motion durations + easing curves + prefers-reduced-motion collapse.
  • Z-index scale (11 bands).
  • Type scale, spacing scale, line heights, letter spacing, border weights.

3. Orphan UIcon shim removed

The Phosphor-backed UIcon slug→glyph lookup at src/ui/primitives/uicon.tsx (introduced in amendment 1 as a transitional shim) had no remaining callers after surface migration. Removed the file and the export { UIcon } from "./uicon"; line from src/ui/primitives/index.ts. Resolves the "obvious next pass" item flagged in HANDOFF_NEXT.md from session #19.

4. Inline argmap-input callsites migrated

Seven inline callsites still used the legacy className="argmap-input" form for native <input> and <select> elements; these are migrated to shadcn primitives or shadcn-Input-equivalent Tailwind classes:

  • src/ui/canvas/canvas-toolbar.tsx — search input now uses shadcn Input from #components/ui/input with overrides h-[26px] w-[140px] bg-muted px-2 text-xs for the floating-toolbar density.
  • src/ui/frame-building/right-pane/editors/term-editor.tsx — Term linked_to picker <select> kept native (for autoFocus/onBlur semantics inline pickers depend on) but restyled with inline Tailwind matching shadcn Input (h-9 w-full rounded-4xl border border-input bg-input/30 px-3 py-1 text-sm + focus/disabled states).
  • src/ui/frame-building/right-pane/editors/interpretation-editor.tsx — Cite Authority picker same treatment.
  • src/ui/frame-building/right-pane/editors/logical-gate-editor.tsx — three native <select>s (gate-type, slot-picker, add-input-picker) same treatment.

The capture-phase keydown handler in frame-canvas.tsx that checks tag === "INPUT" to skip the edge-creation hotkey continues to work since shadcn Input renders a real <input>.

5. argmap-topbar* classes migrated to Tailwind responsive utilities

The four .argmap-topbar* classes in src/ui/chrome/top-bar.tsx (argmap-topbar, argmap-topbar-title, argmap-topbar-chips, argmap-topbar-indicators) are now Tailwind utilities reading from the shadcn theme. The responsive narrow-viewport reflow (§9 #15) is now expressed inline as max-[720px]:hidden (chips), max-[560px]:hidden (indicators), max-[480px]:hidden (title) on the relevant slot divs, with the header itself getting max-[480px]:gap-1 max-[480px]:overflow-x-auto max-[480px]:px-2 for the compact-and-scroll fallback. The four corresponding @media (max-width: …) blocks in global.css are deleted.

6. global.css orphan-rule prune (≈1,500 lines deleted)

The legacy .argmap-* rules whose primitive owners are now shadcn-backed and Tailwind-styled are removed:

  • Buttons: .argmap-btn + all 5 variants (primary, secondary, ghost, destructive, destructive-solid) × hover/active/disabled/loading states; the data-loading="true" spinner overlay; size variants. The shadcn Button primitive owns the resting/hover/active visual now.
  • Icon buttons: the entire .argmap-icon-btn machine — the two-layer outline→solid cross-fade with stack/layer slot machinery, hover bloom, active compression, toggled-on solid-in-accent, subtle/accent variants, reduced-motion override. The shadcn Button size="icon" primitive owns the visual now; the outline↔solid swap is done by Phosphor weight switch on hover in icon-button.tsx.
  • Form controls: .argmap-input + states + the iOS-Safari coarse-pointer 16px zoom guard, .argmap-textarea, .argmap-select (with its inline SVG chevron data URI), .argmap-check, .argmap-radio, .argmap-switch, .argmap-field/-field-label/-field-hint/-field-error/-field-required, .argmap-input-wrap/-input-icon/-input-suffix. shadcn Input/Textarea/Select/Checkbox/RadioGroup/Switch/Label/Alert own these visuals now.
  • Overlays / dialogs: .argmap-overlay + .argmap-dialog + their --exiting states. shadcn Dialog/Sheet/AlertDialog own these now.
  • Misc primitives: .argmap-card (+ hover/active/focus), .argmap-surface, .argmap-output-tab (replaced by raw-button OutputViewTabs per amendment 1's note), .argmap-radio-card[data-active="false"]:hover, .argmap-pill (10 data-tone variants), .argmap-ai-chip (+ ::before radial-gradient dot), .argmap-segmented + .argmap-segmented-tab + .argmap-segmented-item, .argmap-kbd, .argmap-no-scrollbar, .argmap-fullheight, .argmap-number-meta.
  • Dead global selector: the unscoped input[type="radio"|"checkbox"]:not(.argmap-skip-restyle) native-input restyle (matched zero real elements; shadcn Checkbox/RadioGroup render as <button> not <input>).
  • Dead animation keyframes: argmap-dialog-pop-out, argmap-overlay-fade-out, argmap-slide-in-right, argmap-slide-in-left, argmap-fade-in, argmap-node-enter.
  • Focus-visible halo compound selector: trimmed from 8 named primitives (.argmap-card, .argmap-btn, .argmap-icon-btn, .argmap-radio-card, .argmap-output-tab, .argmap-row-hover, .argmap-segmented-tab, .argmap-outline-row[data-focused]) down to two (.argmap-row-hover and .argmap-outline-row[data-focused]) plus the four chrome-chip [data-testid] selectors from §14 #33. shadcn primitives carry their own focus-visible:ring Tailwind classes.

global.css went from 2632 lines to ≈1390 (47% reduction). Canvas-node rules (the five-status whole-node treatment + per-kind accents + edge-trace + handle-glow + focus ring + keyboard-edge-creation candidate ring) are entirely preserved.

  • Article II § 2 (determinism) — UI layer does not invent or reorder canonical compute output. No new LLM use was introduced; existing AI hooks (useAiSuggestion) retain their interface. [data-status] / [data-kind] attribute semantics on canvas nodes are preserved.
  • Article II § 3 (per-node satisfaction criteria) — node-frame.tsx and edge renderers untouched in structure; the canvas-node CSS treatment is entirely preserved.
  • Article II § 4 (independent versioning) — version-history pane already migrated in amendment 1; nothing here touches the repository contracts, version-tree shape, restore + compare flows.
  • Article II § 5 (human-readable structure) — the five-status canvas treatment retained verbatim; the status palette retained; this is the constitutional non-negotiable, and it is honored.
  • Article II § 6 (use-case commitment) — legal and general modes both rendered; mode-accent tokens carry the legal/argument distinction; legal sub-flag chip (binding/persuasive) preserved on Authority nodes.
  • Article III § 2 (legal practice fit) — the consolidated palette reads as one calm, neutral, professional surface across the app, resolving the visible warm-cream / pure-white inconsistency the user identified.
  • Article III § 6 (interface fit) — global.css is 47% smaller; the file is now small enough that a future session can read it without amplifying onboarding cost.
  • Article IV § 6 (non-reversal of user direction) — preserves the user's explicit amendment 1 authorization (shadcn preset, neutral OKLCH, light-only); strengthens it by making the rest of the app actually inherit the preset rather than rendering against a parallel warm palette.
  • Article V § 3 (escalation triggers) — none fire: (a) preserves the user's stated strategy; (b) preserves all Article II commitments; (c) no Article II conflict; (d) no use case foreclosed; (e) no safety implication; (f) constitution not silent — amendment 1 + Stream E v1 govern this directly. The session proceeded autonomously under Article IV § 3.
  • Article XI § 1 (stream immutability) — this amendment was produced rather than editing stream_e_amendment_1.html or stream_e_visual_polish_v1.html; both v1 and amendment 1 remain intact for historical record.
